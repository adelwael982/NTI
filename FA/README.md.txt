This is the full adder made in the four standard ways data flow ,behavioral ,gate level and structural.

Here i made the gate level by looking in the truth table and applying it here. 

whereas the behavioral i simply looked at the behavior of the circuit and applied it in Verilog where it simply adds the two signals. 

The data flow here is similar to the behavioral but its made in a continuous assignment.

The structural is made by making 2 smaller half adders and wired together the half adders could be made in numerous ways but here i stuck the gate level since its a simple design.
