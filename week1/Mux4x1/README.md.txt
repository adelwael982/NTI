This is the Multiplexer made in the four standard ways data flow ,behavioral ,gate level and structural.

Here i made the gate level by mapping the truth table and applying it in tfhe design. 

The behavioral i simply inspected the behavior of the circuit and wrote it in Verilog where it simply choses between 2 signals using the selection line form a if its 0 to d if its 3. 

The data flow here is similar to the behavioral but its made in a continuous assignment.

The structural is made by making 3 smaller multiplexers 2x1 and wired together the mux could be made in numerous ways but here i stuck with the data flow.
Also unlike the rest of the muxes is made from a to d here its the opposite i made thus as a change.
